Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information Mega Payout Paradise ("we") collects when you use megapayoutparadise.online, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and your Google account ID. We do not receive your Google password, and we don't ask Google for access to anything else, such as your contacts, files or mail.
  • Chat messages. The messages, images and details you send in our on-site chat, such as your game username or payment amount, together with basic technical details your browser shares (browser type, language and the page you were on).
  • Payments. Payments are handled by Stripe and PayPal on their own pages. We do not see or store your card or bank details. We only see the payment confirmation they send us.

How we use it

  • To recognise you when you come back, so your chat and its history stay with you on any device.
  • To reply to you, set up your game accounts, load your balance and process cashouts.
  • To keep the service safe and prevent fraud.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your Google information for advertising.

Where it is stored

Chat conversations and the sign-in details above are stored on our own chat server. Only our support team can read them. Payment providers (Stripe, PayPal) keep their own records under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your chat history while you are a player with us so we can help you. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

  • If you'd rather not sign in with Google, you can message us on Facebook Messenger instead.
  • You can sign out at any time from the "Sign in" / account button on the site.
  • To see, correct or delete your information, message us in the chat or on our Facebook page.
  • You can remove our access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Age

Our service is for adults 18 and over only. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Message us in the chat on megapayoutparadise.online or on our Facebook page.
