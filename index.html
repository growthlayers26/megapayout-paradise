FAQ

Questions, answered straight

How do I join?

Message our page. We'll get you set up — no forms, no hoops.

Is there a minimum cashout?

No minimum. You can cash out anytime your balance is above your deposit.

How fast are cashouts?

Up to $500 is processed within 24 hours. Any remaining balance can be cashed out after that.

Which payment methods do you take?

Tap Top up to pay with Cash App Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, or any Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover or Chime debit card. Prefer Venmo or something else? Message us and we'll sort it out.

How do I claim a bonus?

Tap Claim on any offer in Promotions (or just message us). Our team checks your payment and adds the bonus to your game — usually within minutes.

How does refer-a-friend work?

Sign in to get your personal code and share it. When your friend enters it (or opens your link) before their first deposit, and that first deposit is $20 or more, we add $20 for you and the 100% first-deposit bonus for them.

How does the daily streak work?

Each day you deposit, message us to claim that day's reward: $0.25, $0.50, $0.75, $1, $1.50, $2, then $5 on day 7. Skip a day and the streak starts again at day 1.

Do I need to be 18?

Yes — 18+ only (21+ where your region requires it).

How do I get help if something's wrong?

Message us on Facebook anytime and a real person on our team will reply.
